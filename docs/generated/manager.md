## What this notebook does

- The process ingests bank receipts, customer accounts, and open invoices from CSV storage paths. [C6:L1](#c6-l1) [C8:L1](#c8-l1) [C9:L1](#c9-l1) ○
- Receipts are matched against invoices using remittance reference numbers, exact amount matches, or oldest unpaid items first. [C30:L7](#c30-l7) [C30:L9](#c30-l9) [C30:L11](#c30-l11) ○
- When not running in dry-run mode, the engine overwrites the table specified by `tgt` (`fin_cash.alloc_lines`). [C4:L6](#c4-l6) [C37:L1-2](#c37-l1-2) ✓
- An aging snapshot table is updated with current invoice balances using append mode. [C39:L8-9](#c39-l8-9) ○

## The business rules

- Invoices belonging to customer identifiers `C10077` and `C10412` are excluded from matching. [C4:L5](#c4-l5) [C8:L6](#c8-l6) ✓
- Receipts and open items are converted to US Dollars using exchange rates before matching balances. [C18:L3](#c18-l3) [C18:L7](#c18-l7) ○
- Exact matching applies when the difference between the receipt remainder and the open invoice amount is within 0.01. [C4:L3](#c4-l3) [C26:L20](#c26-l20) ✓
- Remaining invoice balances of 5.0 or less are automatically written off. [C26:L48-50](#c26-l48-50) [C29:L4](#c29-l4) ✓
- Receipt funds that cannot be tied to an identified customer account are allocated to suspense. [C30:L16-17](#c30-l16-17) ○
- *external* · Receipt allocations determine General Ledger accounts via the external utility function `get_gl_map`. [C5:L1](#c5-l1) [C33:L3](#c33-l3) ✓

## What could go wrong

- *risk* · The write-off threshold is redefined from 0.5 to 5.0 later in the notebook, which increases automatic write-off exposure tenfold without warning. [C4:L1](#c4-l1) [C29:L4](#c29-l4) ✓
- *risk* · Company reconciliation breaks are detected and printed after data has already been permanently overwritten to disk. [C37:L1-2](#c37-l1-2) [C41:L7-8](#c41-l7-8) ○
- *risk* · Email or message alerts trigger via `notify_ar` even during dry-run runs when nothing is written to ledger tables. [C37:L1](#c37-l1) [C43:L11](#c43-l11) ✓
- *risk* · The pipeline notebook always reports success upon exiting even if reconciliation discrepancies or unmatched receipts occurred. [C47:L1](#c47-l1) ○
- *risk* · Duplicate bank receipt identifiers are detected only with a console printout, allowing duplicated transactions to proceed into allocation. [C19:L9-10](#c19-l9-10) ○
